## Key decisions

**Emboss depth clamp.** After seating on the surface, march back along −n to the
far side and cap the prism's inward reach at 80% of that local thickness — a
thin part (a strap, a 2-unit rod) can no longer get lettering out of its far
surface; thick garments are unchanged (no far side within reach).

**Neck ring vs headSink.** The ring stays at its canon height unless the sunk
chin comes within 0.3 neck-lengths of it, then sits 0.3 neck-lengths below the
chin. `min(...)` of the two keeps every headSink = 0 rig byte-identical.

**Cleats.** Pairs straddle the shoe centreline and an odd last stud is centred,
so the layout is symmetric and both shoes match (the local frame isn't
mirrored per foot, so "mirror per side" would still have needed a centre rule).

**Small validation fixes.** `highlight: 0` means off (like `false`); contour
points are validated as finite `[x, y]` pairs (ValidationError); a font-not-
loaded error from `F.emboss('…')` names emboss and the preload rule, and the
shared glyph error now mentions `F.emboss` too.

**skinRadiusTable cost.** Measured ~56 ms of node construction per windowed hair
(97×49 marches) against a 15–35 s mesh build — not worth a cache; Character
Creator rebuilds create new head nodes anyway, so a per-head cache wouldn't hit.
