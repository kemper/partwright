## Head proxy — `api.headform`

- **Real anthropometry, not the figure rig.** `F.head` / `F.onFace` are
  stylised and unitless. A wearable has to fit a person, so the measurements
  are millimetres (approximate ANSUR II means) in the frame headform
  standards use: origin between the tragions, the Frankfort plane as XY,
  face toward −Y, +X the wearer's left.
- **Options:** S/M/L scale and female/male/neutral presets, and any single
  measurement can be overridden; `circumference` rescales the whole head.
  Combinations that don't describe a head are rejected.
- **Landmarks are snapped onto the surface.** A smooth blend never passes
  exactly through the nominal points. Each one is found by walking out along
  its own direction until the SDF changes sign, so a part placed on a
  landmark touches the skin. The unit test holds every snapped point within
  3 mm of its nominal position for three sizes.
- **Shape:** an SDF blend of ellipsoids and capsules. A sculpt subagent
  smoothed it into one mannequin surface; I kept the nose, eyes, ears and
  the nose-bridge groove, which glasses and masks need.
- **The docs' mask recipe is tested.** The e2e spec runs it verbatim. It
  showed that a 2 mm `api.offset` on a head needs `resolution: 320`, so the
  docs now give the real minimum distances.
- `readDoc("enclosure")` and `readDoc("knurl")` were rejected by the tool
  schema even though ai.md's index lists them. I added both alongside
  `headform`, and a unit test now keeps the readDoc list in step with the
  files in `public/ai/`.
