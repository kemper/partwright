## Head proxy — `api.headform`

- **Real anthropometry, not the figure rig.** `F.head` / `F.onFace` are
  stylised and unitless. A wearable has to fit a person, so the measurements
  are millimetres (approximate ANSUR II means) in the frame headform
  standards use: origin between the tragions, the Frankfort plane as XY,
  face toward −Y, +X the wearer's left.
- **Options:** S/M/L scale and female/male/neutral presets, and any single
  measurement can be overridden; `circumference` rescales the whole head.
  Combinations that don't describe a head are rejected.
- **Landmarks are snapped onto the surface.** A smooth blend never passes
  exactly through the nominal points. Each one is found by walking out along
  its own direction until the SDF changes sign, so a part placed on a
  landmark touches the skin. The unit test holds every snapped point within
  3 mm of its nominal position for three sizes.
- **Shape:** an SDF blend of ellipsoids and capsules. A sculpt subagent
  smoothed it into one mannequin surface; I kept the nose, eyes, ears and
  the nose-bridge groove, which glasses and masks need.
- **The docs' mask recipe is tested.** The e2e spec runs it verbatim. It
  showed that a 2 mm `api.offset` on a head needs `resolution: 320`, so the
  docs now give the real minimum distances.
- `readDoc("enclosure")` and `readDoc("knurl")` were rejected by the tool
  schema even though ai.md's index lists them. I added both alongside
  `headform`, and a unit test now keeps the readDoc list in step with the
  files in `public/ai/`.

## Face rebuilt from survey data

- **The honest answer was no.** The head numbers were recalled ANSUR II means.
  The nose had no data at all: its sizes were fractions of face height. On the
  mesh, the tip stood 11 mm proud instead of ~20, and the nose was a narrow bump
  on a flat face.
- **Sources, computed or quoted, not recalled:**
  - ANSUR II public CSVs (4,082 men, 1,986 women): I computed the head means
    myself. Pupil distance is stored in tenths of a mm.
  - NIOSH 2003 head-and-face survey (Zhuang & Bradtmiller 2005, Table VI),
    which ANSUR II lacks: nose breadth, nose protrusion, subnasale–sellion
    length and jaw width.
  - Neutral is the male/female mean.
- **No fabricated values.** Ear-to-ear breadth has no source in either survey.
  The only number found was 112 mm from a 50-person study, which is
  inconsistent with a ~150 mm skull. Its default is now derived (0.97 ×
  breadth), still overridable, and the docs label it derived.
- **The nose is built from its measurements:**
  - the subnasale sits noseLength from the sellion, and the tip sits
    noseProtrusion from the subnasale, tilted up 25°;
  - the wings' widest points (new alare landmarks) sit in front of the cheek,
    so they can be measured;
  - the midface is set back so the nose stands proud;
  - a broad upper-lip mass carries the subnasale.
- **Pitfalls hit along the way:**
  - a nostril tunnel (genus 1);
  - a groove capsule oriented along Y, which bored pits instead of a crease;
  - lip blends that swallowed the subnasale or widened the wings.
  The probe of achieved values against the measurements caught each one.
- **Tests:** presets equal the survey means; nose relief (dorsum ≥ 10 mm ahead
  of the cheek mid-nose, tip ≥ 20 mm proud); `achieved` covers the nose and
  stays within 5% for three presets.
