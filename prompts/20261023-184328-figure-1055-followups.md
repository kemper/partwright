## Key decisions

**Shag build time — bounding-box culling, not fewer locks.** Profiling a hair-only
model showed shag at ~59 s vs anime ~17 s: ~50 ribbon locks × 9 rotated
ellipsoids were evaluated on every sample of the hair region. Added an internal
`bounded` SDF node (box distance beyond a margin, exact child inside it) with a
compiler emitter, and wrapped each lock. The margin is several times the largest
weld blend, so the mesh is byte-identical (same triangles / volume / area);
shag hair-only dropped to ~16 s and the full Tsubasa figure from ~80 s to ~35 s.

**Temple sliver — a radial window cut.** The sawtooth was the hair's thin
knife-edge wedge where the face-window ellipsoid meets the skull at a grazing
angle, aliasing against the proud painted brows. Clipping the sides with planes
didn't reach the arch corners, so the window cutter is now evaluated after a
radial projection onto the REAL skin (a lat-long lookup of the skin radius,
marched once per hair build): every wall runs toward the head centre, so the
hair ends in a full-thickness step and the window outline on the face is
unchanged. The cutter is culled to its own box (+ a wide margin) so it stays
cheap (its cull margin is a wide 0.6·headZ so thick crown/puff masses still
see the exact cut); a unit test proves the field is unchanged within 2 units of
the surface.
This is a bug fix to the anime/shag cap (no catalog figure uses either).

**Notched fringe.** The shag's fringe layout is computed first and the window
hangs one crisp tooth under each lock, down to that lock's tip (new per-tooth
`azimuths`/`tipHeights` in the notch spec, internal); the fringe ribbon then
lies on its tooth. Neighbouring tips are kept ≥ 0.55 spacing apart so two
jittered locks can't merge into a blob. `notched: false` restores the plain edge.

**Crown spikes** were redesigned rather than scaled: 7 spikes spread round the
back and sides on two root rows, each pushing out along its own surface normal,
so they break the dome's silhouette; `crownSize` scales them. Photo-match showed
very long spikes hurt band 0, so v7 uses `spike` ≈ 0.45.

**Brows in a weld.** `peelTranslation` now searches through union/weld nodes for
the most specific translated child that sits on the rig head; primitives merely
positioned at the head are ignored. Existing `on: skin` figures are unchanged
(verified on expectant_mother).

**Hold solve.** The 'under' contact normal became two extra, softly penalised
search DOFs, so the palm lands on the ball where a short toy arm reaches; the
residual fell from ~24% to ~3-5% of the hand with the contact moving only
~10-15°. 'sides' is untouched. `handScale` feeds the solve and becomes
`F.hands`' default `size`.

**Other choices.** `headSink` is in neck-length units (scale-free, like the other
rig knobs) and moves head/crown/chin/face anchors only, so every head builder
follows. Toy shoes reuse the fitted shoe's sole/collar pipeline with a chunky
body unioned over the foot offset (so skin can't poke through). `F.emboss`
accepts text (Liberation Sans via the existing glyph loader; `emboss(` now
triggers the font preload), CrossSections or raw contours, through a new
internal even-odd polygon-prism SDF; it projects along the surface normal and
cuts the garment grown by `depth`. Fixed `eulerFromBasis`' gimbal lock (x axis
∥ ±Z returned a wrong rotation), found because sideways lettering came out
mis-rotated. Back-compat: four catalog figure examples (expectant_mother,
sneakers, superhero, karate) build byte-identically before and after.
