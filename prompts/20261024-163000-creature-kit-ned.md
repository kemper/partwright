## Key decisions

**Model Ned first, then extract what worked.** I iterated Ned headlessly with
model:preview (about 10 render passes) and kept notes on which techniques fixed
which defect. Each fix became a helper in a new `api.sdf.creature` kit. Ned
was then rewritten on top of the kit and re-verified, so the helpers carry a
recipe that has been through real use rather than a guessed API.

**The defects that the kit removes:**
- Jagged, stair-stepped eye edges. A socket ellipsoid nearly parallel to the
  eyeball grazes it. Fix: a steep-walled aperture that runs deep along the
  view axis, plus an eyeball clipped to the opening (which also stops the
  eyeball poking out the side of the skull).
- A face that reads as a flat mask from 3/4. Fix: one deep skull, with the
  features only 1–2 units proud of it and large blend k. This is a doc rule,
  not a helper.
- An "engraved outline" mouth, and creases tunnelling through to the back of
  the head. Fix: `crease` seats each point on the real outer face via the
  figure's `surfacePoint` (now exported). `dir` is the outward normal; the
  wrong sign had put grooves on the back.
- Flat-plate ears. Fix: a sphere shell clipped to a tapered leaf outline, with
  a labelled inner membrane and raised veins.
- `build` attaches features, clips flat soles, labels the skin and gathers
  every feature's detail sphere, because forgetting detail regions is a
  common reason faces look faceted.

**Topology traps found in browser verification.** Genus in the hundreds came
from three sources:
- thin hard-unioned vein strips (now chunkier and smooth-welded);
- a near-coplanar membrane/shell overlap (the membrane now sits about 0.5 t
  into the shell);
- a large ear grazing the cheeks/shoulders (example tuned; documented as a
  gotcha).

A plinth whose top face sat exactly at z = 0 left zero-volume slivers against
the flat soles. The example raises the plinth top by 0.4. All are documented
in creature.md.

**Placement.** `sdfCreature.ts` mirrors `sdfFigure`'s layering: it imports
nothing from `sdf.ts` and is wired in by `createSdfNamespace`, so the module
graph stays acyclic. It's a new `readDoc('creature')` subdoc, routed from the
system prompt, ai.md, sdf.md and figure.md. A spec-driven Character-Creator
equivalent for creatures was considered and deferred. The kit plus a full
worked example is a smaller first step that already covers the failure modes.

**Ned is an example, not a catalog entry.** It is a fan model of a band's
character, so it lives in `examples/` and the doc. Adding it to the public
catalog is the owner's call.
