## Liked / Worked
- `model:preview` with `--view "az,el;…"` plus a natively cropped 1100 px
  render made ~10 sculpt passes cheap (~20–30 s each). The 3/4 view (az −50)
  caught the "face is a flat mask" and "eyeball pokes out of the skull"
  defects that front and side both hid.
- Building one real subject first and only then extracting helpers meant
  every helper default came from a configuration that had already been
  through review.

## Lacked
- `model:preview` reports `genus` but nothing flags it. Genus 130–250 from
  hair-thin hard-union overlaps (ear veins, membrane vs shell, an ear grazing
  the shoulders) only surfaced when the browser run showed
  `componentCount 2` / `containedComponents 1`. That took ~6 turns to bisect.
- Playwright clears `test-results/` on every run, which deleted the composed
  before/after PNGs I had saved there. Keep proof images in the scratchpad.

## Learned
- Hard-unioning two separately-labelled SDF regions whose surfaces are nearly
  coplanar, or overlap by less than ~1 grid cell, marches into tunnels (genus
  in the hundreds). Overlap by ≥ 0.5 × thickness, or smooth-weld inside one
  label.
- A plinth whose top face sits exactly on the ground plane leaves zero-volume
  slivers against flat SDF soles. Sink the soles ~0.4 into the base.
- `F.surfacePoint` only searches within `point ± dir·reach`, so a 2-D `[x, z]`
  projection must start from the surface's bbox centre, not from y = 0.

## Longed for
- A `model:preview` warning when `genus` jumps (e.g. > 20 for a figure), plus
  `--explain-genus`, which bisects by label region to show which part carries
  the handles.
- A `--matrix` flag that sweeps one parameter across values and tabulates
  `componentCount` / `genus` / triangles. I hand-rolled shell loops for this
  three times.
