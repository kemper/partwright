5. **[docs precision] The NUL-byte `main.ts` guidance doesn't say the dedicated `Grep` tool has no `-a` flag** — 1 session burned a wasted `Grep -a` call before falling back to Bash's `grep -an`. Small, crisp, worth a one-line fix even at one occurrence. **Fixed.**

7. **[testing] Rail/list drag e2e silently misses off-screen rows** — 1 session (part-grouping), 2 wasted runs before realizing the parts-list scroll box clips drag targets even at the config's 1280×900 default. **Fixed** — added to `docs/playwright-guide.md`'s AI-agent-gotchas list, next to the existing viewport-size note it extends.
