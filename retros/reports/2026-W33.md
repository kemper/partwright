6. **[tooling, backlog, already tracked] Scatter/deform API friction — reinforced 4x by a 16-agent batch.** `2026-07-20-catalog-refresh-agent-feedback.md` aggregates 16 independent rework agents applying the new Blender-parity verbs to existing catalog entries.
