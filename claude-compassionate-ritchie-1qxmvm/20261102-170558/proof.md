**A sculpted figure, cut with `splitIntoPieces`.** A 3.75M-triangle figure, simplified to about 400k, was cut into 11 pieces by an agent driving the real app. Detect parts → accept → `splitIntoPieces` (plug eyes, medial arms and tail, flat neck and legs, 0.15 mm clearance; the fins are separate shells, trimmed to the ears). Every piece is a single watertight solid, no two overlap, and each slides out of its neighbour.
